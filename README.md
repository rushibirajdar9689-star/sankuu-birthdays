# A Little World for Sankuu 🎀

> A dreamy, romantic, kawaii-style birthday website crafted with infinite love for **Sankruti (Sankuu)** on her special day! 🎂💖

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎀 Welcome Opening Screen:** Full-screen greeting with animated button "OPEN YOUR SURPRISE 🎀" that seamlessly reveals the website.
- **👑 Birthday Hero Section:** Dedicated to Sankruti with sparkling letters, golden crown, floating balloon bouquets, and romantic birthday message.
- **🎂 Interactive 3-Tier Strawberry Cake:** Layered CSS cake with strawberries, ruffles, and 3 glowing candles that blow out with smoke puffs and confetti when you click **"MAKE A WISH, POOKIE! ✨"**, plus a relight option.
- **💌 Love Letter Experience:** Animated 3D envelope with a wax seal that opens to reveal a handwritten letter with smooth typewriter-style text reveal. (Easily editable in `letter.js`).
- **❤️ Heart-Shaped Photo Gallery:** Unmistakable heart-shaped polaroid collage featuring personal memories, complete with washi tapes, pins, and a full-screen lightbox with smooth navigation.
- **🎶 Aesthetic Music Section:** Vinyl record player with spinning disc, tonearm, sound visualizers, and sweet romantic background music.
- **🧸 Bubu & Dudu Sweet Moments:** Authentic Bubu and Dudu animations throughout the website illustrating cute romantic scenes (hugging, holding hands, giving flowers, sitting in hearts, celebrating with cake, blowing kisses).
- **✨ Ambient Magic:** Floating pastel clouds, interactive heart cursor trails, sound effects, and celebratory confetti.

## 💌 How to Edit the Love Letter

Open [`letter.js`](./letter.js) in any text editor and edit the text inside `window.POOKIE_LETTER`. Save the file, and your personalized letter will automatically display on the website!
